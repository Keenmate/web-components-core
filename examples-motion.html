Motion level

One switch for how much every component animates: motion="auto | none | snappy | smooth" on an element, window.components.motion for the page, and data-motion on any element (a section, <html>) for everything inside. Components follow through the shared --base-duration-* / --base-scroll-behavior tokens, with or without base-css-variables on the page. The OS reduced-motion setting always wins. See SPEC §12.16.

MO02 · Page-wide and per section

window.components.motion applies to every component left on auto. data-motion on a wrapper applies to everything inside it, even without base-css-variables (core adds the presets once a component connects). A component's own motion always beats both. The last knob below keeps motion="smooth" to show that.
